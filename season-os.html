Leading indicators

Not vanity numbers — these are the inputs that decide whether the four bets compound. Distribution is a leading indicator: reach built now is what makes the interviews, the events and the invitations possible later.

The tape, not the scale

For a swimwear-model line the variable is ratio, not mass — shoulder against waist, waist against hip. Muscle you're adding to 肩/背/臀 will hide on the scale and show on the tape. Measure once a week, same morning, before food.

The physique program

肩 · 背 · 臀 撑起来，腰留窄 —

Waist-safe core 腰不能练粗

Anti-movement and bracing, not loaded side bends or heavy weighted twists — those build obliques outward and thicken the line you're trying to keep narrow. Daily, 8 minutes, after training.

体态 · posture block

The fastest-moving lever you have. Desk hours and running both pull you into rounded shoulders and a tucked-under rib cage; a model line is thoracic extension, stacked ribs over pelvis, shoulders back and down. 10 minutes, most days.

Sunday story

这周是一个什么样的故事 —

A week isn't a score, it's a scene. Write it as one — an opening, a turn, and the thing you now know that you didn't on Monday. Ten quiet minutes, no phone.

Your data

全部存档 —

Everything is kept, not summarised — every cycle with its target and result, every morning's full text, every touch with every person, every weigh-in. Pages are stored one file per month so a long season never hits a size ceiling. Nothing is ever overwritten by a rollup.
